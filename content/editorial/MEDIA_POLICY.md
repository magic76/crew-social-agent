# Media choice and handling

Choose the medium after the story is selected. Text-only is deliberate and valid. A media file is not part of the publication handoff until it exists, its provenance is documented, and the normal Queue checks pass.

## Social image standards

Follow the image section in [the canonical editorial guidelines](../../docs/editorial-guidelines.md). An image must show the story's key scene or conflict with a clear setting, details, and visual hierarchy; avoid a lone symbolic object. Add one short Traditional Chinese headline that reads at a glance on a phone. Use portrait 4:5 at 1080×1350, with safe margins and high contrast; do not rely on tiny text. Crew Cloud is optional and, when used, a supporting character. No fake screenshots, fake data, invented test results, or ad-like framing. Record generated conceptual art as an illustration, not as a product capture, and describe its provenance and limits accurately in alt text.

Before handoff, view the image at phone-feed size, then cover the headline or ignore the lettering and ask: **「不看文字，這張圖能讓人猜到主題嗎？」** If the scene does not communicate the event or conflict without the words, revise it, choose genuine shareable evidence, or use text-only.

## Evidence-first visual priority (2026-11-15)

For hands-on DIY content or everyday AI makeovers, prefer the **actual output** over an attractive AI illustration: genuine device/control photos, a real website/game on a phone, safe before/after views, or a short clip of the demonstrated action. For tutorials, show the finished outcome and one or two real milestone steps rather than a wall of fake UI. Protect personal data, third-party imagery and credentials, and obtain any needed permissions. A generated concept image must never be labeled as the creator's finished work. If no shareable evidence exists, use a clearly labeled illustration or text-only; don't fabricate completion to secure a more clickable picture.

## Media by story type

| Story type | Preferred evidence or treatment | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Real development, bug, test, or product | Genuine app capture, relevant diff, actual error/output, or a before/after made from real captures. Redact secrets and personal data. | Generated mock screens, fabricated test output, or claims that a commit message proves a successful test. |
| Funny surprise or awkward interaction | A short comic/storyboard with one clear event, expressive character, and readable visual punchline. | Generic smiling robot, decorative AI brain, floating cards with no story. |
| AI research/news | Small accurate mechanism diagram, a source-grounded editorial illustration, or no image. | A generic purple “AI” aura or a diagram that implies evidence/results not in the source. |
| Dense post with no visual payoff | Text-only. | Generate art solely to meet a quota. |

Keep Crew Cloud only when it strengthens the scene, and vary pose, framing, emotion, location and art medium. The social image standards do not replace the Crew Light v1 video style or the video handoff contract; video keeps its separate aspect ratio, production rules, assets and QA. Store asset path, type, creator/source, rights/permission note, alt text and a one-sentence reason in the review record. Generated illustrations must never be presented as actual product captures.
