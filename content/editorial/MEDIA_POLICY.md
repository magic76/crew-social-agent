# Media choice and handling

Choose the medium after the story is selected. `text-only` is deliberate and valid. A media file is not part of the publication handoff until the file exists, its provenance is documented, and the normal Queue checks pass.

| Story type | Preferred evidence or treatment | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Real development, bug, test, or product | Genuine app capture, relevant diff, actual error/output, or a before/after made from real captures. Redact secrets and personal data. | Generated mock screens, fabricated test output, or claims that a commit message proves a successful test. |
| Funny surprise or awkward interaction | A short comic/storyboard with one clear event, expressive character, and readable visual punchline. | Generic smiling robot, decorative AI brain, floating cards with no story. |
| AI research/news | Small accurate mechanism diagram, a source-grounded editorial illustration, or no image. | A generic purple “AI” aura or a diagram that implies evidence/results not in the source. |
| Dense post with no visual payoff | Text-only. | Generate art solely to meet a quota. |

Keep Crew Cloud when it strengthens the scene, but vary pose, framing, emotion, location and art medium. The old Crew Light v1 rules continue to apply to video production only; the new social-image policy does not silently replace the video handoff contract. Store asset path, type, creator/source, rights/permission note, alt text and a one-sentence reason in the review record. Generated illustrations must be labeled as illustrations, never presented as actual product captures.
