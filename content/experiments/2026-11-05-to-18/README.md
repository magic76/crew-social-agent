# Crew Lab SI editorial pilot · 2026-11-05—2026-11-18

## Purpose

Test whether evidence-backed development stories and varied media produce more worthwhile reading and interaction than the previous news-summary-heavy pattern. Daily research continues separately. A day may produce zero, one or two posts; do not fill the cap.

## Pilot rules

- 14 consecutive days: 2026-11-05 through 2026-11-18 inclusive. Review the process on 2026-11-19. Use only actual posts and actual observations.
- Initial content-mix direction across the window: about 60% development/bug/project story, 30% practical technique or discussion, 10% AI news/research. These are descriptive targets, not quotas.
- Keep the existing Facebook/Threads Queue and Local Publisher contracts. The editorial pilot does not grant publication authorization. Do not alter past or already authorized Queue items.
- Preserve each post's platform, actual publish time, topic class, copy-length band, media type, source, and status. Facebook and Threads are separate observations for the same story, not independent editorial topics.
- Do not vary every factor at once. Prefer natural comparisons within the same platform and similar topic class. Record when no comparable pair exists. Never create duplicate/filler posts solely to make an A/B pair.
- Compare development story vs AI news, short vs standard copy, real screenshot vs story comic vs diagram vs text-only, and Facebook vs Threads. For each comparison, report counts and raw observed values; do not claim causal lift or statistical confidence from this small exploratory sample.

## Measures

Primary measures when the platform provides them: non-follower reach, comments, shares, new followers, website clicks, and new followers per 1,000 non-follower reach. Add publication URL/time and measurement time. Prefer a consistent observation window (for example 24 hours after publication) and mark the actual age of every observation. Do not derive a rate when its denominator is absent or zero.

The blank `metrics.csv` is for manually entered observations. `pending_local_publisher` means there is no confirmed publication yet. If analytics are unavailable, leave cells blank and mark `manual_data_needed`; do not substitute likes or total reach without labeling the field. Review delayed 7-day observations on 2026-11-26 or later.

## Analysis

At the end of the window, summarize how many candidates were reviewed, selected, rejected and held; how many selected items actually published; missing-data rate; and descriptive per-platform measures grouped by topic, length and media type. Include sample counts and ranges. Separate an observed pattern from a causal explanation. One strong or weak post is a case note, not a learned rule.

## Manual tracker

Append one row per published post per platform to `metrics.csv` only after verifying publication. Leave not-yet-known fields blank.
