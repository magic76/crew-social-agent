# Crew Lab SI editorial pilot · 2026-11-05—2026-11-18

## Purpose

Test whether evidence-backed development stories and varied media produce more worthwhile reading and interaction than the previous news-summary-heavy pattern. Daily research continues separately. Starting with the 2026-11-05 run, prepare at least six distinct topic drafts each day, one for each planned time slot; each topic has separate Facebook and Threads copy. The number actually published may be lower because publication still depends on explicit authorization and Local Publisher conditions.

## Pilot rules

- 14 consecutive days: 2026-11-05 through 2026-11-18 inclusive. Review the process on 2026-11-19. Use only actual posts and actual observations. The 2026-11-05 batch was expanded to six topics after the owner clarified the daily minimum; the six-topic target applies from 2026-11-05 onward.
- Search credible online primary sources to fill gaps in owned-project stories. Keep each article tied to verifiable evidence and do not invent personal testing or first-person development experiences.
- Initial content-mix direction across the window: about 60% development/bug/project story, 30% practical technique or discussion, 10% AI news/research. These are descriptive targets, not quotas.
- Keep the existing Facebook/Threads Queue and Local Publisher contracts. The editorial pilot does not grant publication authorization. Do not alter past or already authorized Queue items.
- Preserve each post's platform, actual publish time, topic class, copy-length band, media type, `product_mentioned` (true/false from the actual copy), source, and status. Facebook and Threads are separate observations for the same story, not independent editorial topics. A product-specific name/link counts; a first-reply source citation alone does not.
- Do not vary every factor at once. Prefer natural comparisons within the same platform and similar topic class. Record when no comparable pair exists. The six daily topics must be distinct and pass the editorial gate; never duplicate or fabricate content solely to fill a slot.
- Compare development story vs AI news, short vs standard copy, real screenshot vs story comic vs diagram vs text-only, Facebook vs Threads, and posts with vs without a site/product mention. For mention comparisons, group by platform and use only verified published rows; report sample counts and raw observed reach, new follows, and website clicks. Do not claim causal lift or statistical confidence from this small exploratory sample.

## Editorial phase change: 2026-11-15

At the owner's request, the editorial direction has been revised toward practical beginner creations, real-life AI improvements, and interesting verified experiments. From 2026-11-15, a **hypothesis to test**, not a hard quota: approximately 40% beginner-friendly how-to/making, 30% everyday AI makeover, 20% experiments and development discoveries, 10% useful news/research, subject to evidence and quality. The original 60/30/10 direction above describes the period before this amendment and must not be retrospectively rewritten. Compare pre-change and post-change observations with dates and platform/post age, label samples as exploratory, and avoid attributing reach differences solely to the new editorial strategy.

For the two owner-reported stronger posts (LG TV app and beginner GitHub Pages tutorial), record them as **qualitative leads**, not observed metric rows without confirmed publication identifiers and platform data. Use genuine shareable results where possible, and track whether a post offers an actionable takeaway versus merely summarizing technology. The existing metrics schema, publication permissions, and Queue rules are unchanged.

## Measures

Primary measures when the platform provides them: non-follower reach, comments, shares, new followers, website clicks, and new followers per 1,000 non-follower reach. Add publication URL/time and measurement time. Prefer a consistent observation window (for example 24 hours after publication) and mark the actual age of every observation. Do not derive a rate when its denominator is absent or zero.

The blank `metrics.csv` is for manually entered observations. `pending_local_publisher` means there is no confirmed publication yet. If analytics are unavailable, leave cells blank and mark `manual_data_needed`; do not substitute likes or total reach without labeling the field. Review delayed 7-day observations on 2026-11-26 or later.

## Analysis

At the end of the window, summarize how many candidates were reviewed, selected, rejected and held; how many selected items actually published; missing-data rate; and descriptive per-platform measures grouped by topic, length and media type. Include sample counts and ranges. Separate an observed pattern from a causal explanation. One strong or weak post is a case note, not a learned rule.

Append one row per published post per platform to `metrics.csv` only after verifying publication. Leave not-yet-known fields blank.
